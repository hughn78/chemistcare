## B. Scope & service description

**In the document's own words (p.4):**
> "This Protocol has been developed to provide pharmacists authorised under the Drugs, Poisons and Controlled Substances Regulations 2017 (the Regulations) a clear framework to supply the Schedule 4 poisons documented in this Protocol for the initiation of the oral contraceptive pill under a structured prescribing arrangement. It is a requirement of the Secretary Approval: Community Pharmacist Program that pharmacists comply with this Protocol when supplying Schedule 4 poisons for the initiation of the oral contraceptive pill. It is also a requirement of the Secretary Approval: Community Pharmacist Program that pharmacists have completed the current training requirements specified in the departmental guidance before supplying Schedule 4 poisons."

**Scope limit (p.5, flowchart):**
> "The scope of this service is limited to pharmacist-led commencement or restarting of an oral contraceptive pill only."

**Purpose-of-use limit (p.5, flowchart gate question):**
> "Is the patient requesting a contraceptive medicine for contraceptive purposes?" — a "No" answer routes to "Refer to General Practitioner."

**Setting requirement (p.5–6, flowchart):**
> "Is the private consultation room available to conduct the service?" → No: "Do not proceed until room is available"
> "*The patient must be physically present at the pharmacy."

**Explicitly OUT of scope — contraceptive methods (p.23):**
> "The following hormonal contraceptives have been excluded from the Program:
> • COCPs with a high estrogen dose (50 micrograms of ethinylestradiol or equivalent) - not routinely recommended for contraception due to unacceptable risk of VTE
> • Combined hormonal vaginal ring
> • Depot medroxyprogesterone (DMPA) injection
> • Etonogestrel subdermal contraceptive implant
> • Levonorgestrel-releasing intrauterine contraceptive device"

**Out of scope but must still be offered/discussed (p.6):** "All available contraceptive options, including options not available through this service e.g. LARCs, combined hormonal vaginal ring and DMPA injection."

**Out of scope — other (p.7):** "The patient requests or requires a method of contraception that is not available in the Oral Contraceptive Service" (exclusion criterion).

**Transgender / non-binary scope limit (p.14):**
> "This service supports the initiation of oral contraception for contraceptive purposes within the clinical parameters of this protocol. Transgender and non-binary people receiving gender-affirming hormone therapy, or who have more complex hormonal management needs, may require referral to their regular medical practitioner or an appropriately experienced sexual health or gender-affirming healthcare service for comprehensive, culturally safe care tailored to their individual needs."

**Professional obligations (p.4):** must operate per the Act/Regulations and "all other applicable Victorian, Commonwealth and national laws"; "At all times act in a manner consistent with the Pharmacy Board of Australia's (the Board) Code of Conduct". Also: "Pharmacists are also expected to exercise professional judgment in adapting treatment guidelines to presenting circumstances."
